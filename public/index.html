How to Use

1

Export from Meta Ads Manager

Open Meta Ads Manager. Switch to the Ads view (not campaigns or ad sets). Set your date range to the past 7 days. Click Export → Export Table Data and download as CSV or Excel. To get ad thumbnails, make sure to include the Preview URL column in your export.

2

Upload the file

On the Brief tab, drag and drop your exported file into the upload area on the left, or click to browse. The app will automatically read all your ad names, spend, ROAS, CTR and other metrics.

3

Generate the brief

Once your file is loaded, click Generate brief. The AI will analyse your ads and produce a plain-English brief in a few seconds - no metrics knowledge needed.

4

Read the brief

The brief is split into five sections: Top performers (scale these), Make next (new ideas based on what's working), Fatigue alerts (showing ad exhaustion), Underperformers (fix or pause), and Retire now (kill immediately).

5

Leave comments

The whole team can read the brief and leave comments - feedback, ideas, questions. Scroll to the bottom of the brief to post. Comments are saved and visible to everyone.

6

Export as PDF

Click Export PDF in the top right of the brief to print or save it as a PDF. Use this to share in Slack, attach to briefs, or save for records. Comments and the sidebar won't appear in the PDF.

💡

Browsing ad data and thumbnails

Go to the Data tab to see all your ads. Switch to Cards view to see ad thumbnails (if your export includes a preview URL). Click any card to open a full detail view with the creative image and all metrics - ROAS, Spend, CTR, CPC, CPM, Clicks, Impressions, Reach and Frequency.

💡

Using Google Sheets instead

If you paste your Meta data into a shared Google Sheet, publish it as CSV (File → Share → Publish to web → CSV), then paste that URL into the Google Sheet tab on the left. The app fetches it automatically - no file upload needed.

💡

View-only link for the team

Share https://adbrief.onrender.com?role=summary with the team. This hides the upload controls so they can only read and comment - not accidentally change anything.

💡

Deleting old briefs

Go to the History tab and hover over any brief. A red Delete button will appear - click it to permanently remove that week's brief. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

⏱

Slow to load?

The app runs on a free server that goes to sleep after 15 minutes of no activity. The first visit of the day may take up to 30 seconds to load - just wait and it will come back. Once it's awake it's fast.
